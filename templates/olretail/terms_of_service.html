This is a working draft describing how TimorMart actually operates today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer qualified in Timor-Leste, and should be before it's relied on as a binding legal document.

1. What TimorMart Is

TimorMart is an online marketplace that connects independent buyers, sellers, and delivery couriers across Timor-Leste. TimorMart does not manufacture, own, or sell the products listed on the platform — each product is listed and sold by an independent seller, and each delivery is carried out by an independent courier. By using TimorMart, you agree to these terms.

2. Accounts

You can register as a Buyer, a Seller, a Courier, or both a Buyer and Seller on one account. You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide and for keeping your login details secure. One account per person — don't create multiple accounts to get around a suspension or a platform rule.

Courier accounts must submit a photo of a valid ID and driving license, and are only assigned deliveries once an administrator has verified those documents.

3. Listings

Every product a seller submits is reviewed by an administrator before it becomes visible to buyers, and can be rejected, sent back for changes, or suspended later if it breaks these terms (for example: prohibited items, misleading pricing or condition, or counterfeit goods). Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of their own listings — the price, description, condition (new or second-hand), and photos.

4. Payment Methods & Fees

TimorMart currently supports two ways to pay at checkout:

  • Cash on Delivery — you pay the courier or seller directly, in cash, when your order arrives. You pay only the item price plus a flat $1.00 delivery fee. TimorMart never holds or handles this money.
  • Bank Transfer — you transfer the full amount to TimorMart's own bank account before the order ships. This total includes a 2% platform service fee on top of the item price, plus the $1.00 delivery fee. TimorMart holds these funds and pays the seller once delivery is confirmed.

Card payments are not currently available. Sellers receive the full listed price of each item they sell once it's delivered — the platform's fee on Bank Transfer orders is charged to the buyer, not deducted from the seller.

5. Delivery

Orders are delivered by a courier matched to the buyer's city, or arranged directly by the seller. The courier confirms delivery with a photo, and both the product and the courier can be rated by the buyer afterward. Estimated delivery timing is set by the seller and isn't guaranteed by TimorMart.

6. Cancellations

You can cancel an order yourself only before payment has been confirmed — that is, while it's still awaiting payment or awaiting confirmation of a bank transfer you've reported. Once a payment is confirmed, an order can no longer be self-cancelled; see the Refund Policy below for what happens if something goes wrong after that point.

7. Disputes

If an order arrives damaged, wrong, or never arrives, you can open a dispute on that order. The seller has 3 days to respond. An administrator reviews the dispute — including any evidence submitted — and makes the final decision, which may include a refund (see the Refund Policy).

For Bank Transfer orders, a buyer's claim that they've sent payment is verified directly by an administrator against TimorMart's actual bank statement, not by the seller.

8. Prohibited Conduct

You may not use TimorMart to:

  • List illegal, counterfeit, stolen, or unsafe goods
  • Post fake reviews or ratings, or manipulate another user's rating
  • Submit a fraudulent payment claim, reused receipt, or fake delivery/ID document
  • Harass, threaten, or discriminate against another user
  • Attempt to circumvent an account suspension by creating a new account

9. Suspension & Termination

TimorMart may suspend or remove a listing, or restrict or close an account, for breaking these terms — including fraud, repeated unverifiable payment claims, or prohibited listings. Where reasonably possible, you'll be told why.
